Control room · PC

Control Room

Everything the operator does: set the beach this installation watches, add cameras and draw their danger zones, watch the wall with live risk overlays, work the active incidents with their evidence, test recorded footage through the full detection chain, check that lifeguard phones are reachable, and read the response metrics.
Lifeguard · phone

Lifeguard Alerts

One screen per alert: the evidence frame, where it is, and one big Acknowledge button. Alarm, vibration and background alerts are configurable, and it installs to a phone's home screen so it rings with the app closed.

What this is

Decision support, not an autonomous authority. OceanEye raises candidate events from interpretable rules (danger zone + behaviour signals + persistence) on a general-purpose person detector. A human confirms every one. Thresholds are experimental and not validated against labelled drowning footage.
